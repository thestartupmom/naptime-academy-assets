First, what's a skill?

Think of a skill like a recipe card for Claude. You add it once, and every time that job comes up, Claude follows the recipe the same great way, without you typing out your instructions every time.

You don't have to remember a special command, either. Just ask for the thing, like "caption this reel," and Claude pulls out the right recipe card on its own.

easy peasy.

Skill 1 of 7 · Writing

Captions that sound like you.

Tell it about your post, and it hands you one caption in your voice, ready to paste. No "Here's your caption!", no list of ten options, nothing to delete first.

Download Caption
  • About 2 minutes to add
  • Works on every Claude plan, even the free one
  • Use it in the Claude app, Cowork, or Claude Code
How to add it ↓

Why I love it

I use this one all the time. I film, I type "caption this," I paste, I post. It puts the hook first (that's the line people see before they tap "more"), adds something your post doesn't already say, and ends with one clear ask, like "comment GUIDE and I'll send it to you." It even skips the stuff that makes a caption sound like AI wrote it.

The rules

Use it the right way.

Public info only

Never private accounts, and never personal info that isn't posted publicly.

Study, don't copy

Learn the pattern, then make it yours. Never repost someone else's words.

Be kind with contacts

Send one personal message someone would be glad to get, instead of a mass email.

Save what matters

Apify deletes results after 7 days, so ask Claude for a spreadsheet of anything you want to keep.
